# Perfect-Day

## Description

Welcome to MACintosh, a Windows-inspired personal journal website about our perfect day. The website follows our journey through the day while we describe the best aspects of it. From morning to night, we enjoy food and activities until we all reunite together at a night market/festival. We incorporated multiple interactive activities throughout the website, including sound, touch, and a voting platform at the end. We made sure to also add in our own silly personalities to make this website beyond unique.

## How our project was created

First, we brainstormed together about what our perfect days would be like, and we compiled a list of our perfect day activities in a document. Afterward, we initially decided to create a visual novel going through a person's perfect day with all of our ideas together. But after further consideration, we decided that we instead wanted to create a website that shows each of our perfect days in the style of a WEB OS inspired by the old Windows computer. We wanted to make it feel as if one was scrolling; they were following the selected person's perfect day while the background colour changes depending on the time. After loads of research and time brainstorming, and obviously coding, we finally created MACintosh, a journal that contains all three team members' perfect days.
